# Renting a taxi car or using your own: which pays more in 2026

![Renting a taxi car or using your own: which pays more in 2026](https://car-city.pro/themes/img/kopiya-1-(3).png)

You don't have to buy a separate car to work in a taxi: you can use your own or rent a suitable model.

## What costs affect your taxi earnings

The total of completed orders shows the driver's revenue, not what they actually earn. Part of the money goes on required and day-to-day costs.

When you calculate, take into account:

- the service's and the taxi fleet's commission, if there is one;
- fuel or charging;
- taxes;
- car washes and other costs during the shift;
- the rental payment or the cost of keeping your own car.

Drivers may have similar types of costs, but the amounts differ. The result depends on the fare class, schedule, number of orders, mileage without a passenger and even the area you work in.

In Moscow you also need to consider the terms of the service that sends you orders. For example, Yandex's commission depends on the fare class you choose. So comparing different drivers' income only by daily revenue is not correct.

## Your own car: when it can pay off

If you already own a suitable car, you have one less big daily cost: the rent. If you only work now and then, this is a big advantage: you don't pay for the car on days between shifts.

But taxi work changes how the car is used. Mileage builds up faster, so maintenance and replacement of consumables come sooner. The brakes, suspension and tyres take more strain. The busier your schedule, the faster these costs show up.

There are also costs that are hard to see in a single month's budget. A car with high mileage loses value over time. And if it needs repairs, the owner has to pay for them and is left without a work car for a while.

Finally, not every personal car is suitable for the work you plan. Aggregators have their own requirements for models and fare classes. For example, when classifying a car Yandex looks at, among other things, its model, age and average market value.

## A rented car: what the driver pays for

With rental, the economics are different. Instead of buying a car and paying the costs after that, you have a regular payment known in advance.

The driver doesn't have to find money to buy a suitable model. You can rent a car of the right class and start working without using your personal car for daily trips with passengers.

Still, study the rental terms before signing the contract. It's not only the daily price that matters: find out how maintenance is organised, who pays for which repairs, what the rules are after an accident and whether you get a replacement car if yours breaks down.

The regular payment remains the main factor to consider when planning your schedule. The fewer shifts you work, the bigger its share of costs compared with your revenue. That's why rental usually shows its advantages with steady work, not with a few random shifts a month.

## How much you can earn in a taxi on a rented car

Even two taxi drivers with the same schedule can get different results because of the fare class, demand, routes and number of trips without passengers.

Say the orders completed during a shift added up to 10 000 rubles. On its own, this figure says nothing about how much the driver keeps. You need to subtract commissions, rent for that period, fuel, tax and other work costs.

So when choosing a car, it makes sense to start from your own schedule. For example, work out your costs for 15, 20 and 25 shifts a month in advance and figure out what average daily revenue you need to get the result you want.

## How to earn more on a rented car

You can improve your results not only by working longer shifts. It is just as important for a driver to keep costs under control and cut the time when the car is in use but not earning.

First of all, keep an eye on a few things:

- Demand. The number of orders changes with the hour and day. Take into account rush hours, weather, weekends and big events.
- Mileage without a passenger. The more such kilometres, the higher your fuel costs for the same revenue.
- Fuel consumption. With high monthly mileage, even a small difference in consumption noticeably affects the final amount.
- Fare class. A higher car class can raise the price of individual trips, but compare it with demand and the rental cost.
- Shift results. If you write down your revenue and main costs, after a few weeks it becomes easier to find the most effective schedule.

Drivers taking orders through Yandex should go by the current situation in Yandex Pro: demand changes during the day, so there is no single way of working that suits every time and area.

## Which option suits which situation

The choice depends not only on the cost of one shift but also on what car the driver already has, how regularly they plan to work and whether they are ready to handle maintenance themselves.

Your own car usually makes sense if you already have a suitable one and you don't plan constant, intensive shifts. Rental becomes more attractive when the driver doesn't want to buy a separate car for taxi work, add mileage to their own car or deal with all the maintenance themselves.
