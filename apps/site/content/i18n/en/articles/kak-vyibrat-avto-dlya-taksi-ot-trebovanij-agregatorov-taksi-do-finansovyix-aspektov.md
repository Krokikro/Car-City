# How to choose a taxi car: from taxi aggregator requirements to the financial side

![How to choose a taxi car](https://car-city.pro/themes/imgs/banners/5.jpg)

Taxi work today is a solid option for anyone looking for a way to earn. But success in this field depends a lot on choosing the right car. A wrong step can lead to needless costs and lower profits.

### Key factors when choosing a rental car for taxi work

1. Taxi aggregator requirements: each aggregator has its own internal classification that sets fare classes and limits on which cars are allowed. For example, in Moscow and the Moscow region cars made before 2009 are not allowed on the "Economy" and UberX fare classes. Check the aggregators' requirements in your region to avoid unpleasant surprises.
2. Technical condition: the car you rent for taxi work must be in perfect condition, because daily heavy use puts a lot of strain on it. Check all of the car's systems, from the engine and transmission to the brakes and suspension.
3. Economy: choose models with low fuel consumption to cut running costs. Pay special attention to cars with modern petrol engines or new-generation diesel engines.
4. Maintenance cost: take into account the cost of regular maintenance and repairs if they aren't included in the taxi fleet's services. Cars that are cheap to maintain and use common parts available on the market will cut your costs. Carcity does maintenance and necessary repairs of its fleet at its own expense.
5. Reliability: prefer makes and models known for being long-lasting and easy to maintain. Remember that downtime because of breakdowns means direct losses for a taxi driver.
6. Comfort: comfort matters for both the driver and the passengers. Roomy seats, a comfortable driving position and good sound insulation are important for comfortable work.
7. Capacity: a roomy interior and boot are important for taxi work. Ideally the car should fit large luggage.
8. Financial side: renting a taxi car is a serious investment. Work out all the costs: not only the rental price of the car and the expected profit, but also insurance, fuel, taxes and depreciation.

### What else to consider:

1. Before buying a car, carefully study its main specifications and other taxi drivers' reviews of the model.
2. Take a test drive to check the car's comfort and handling.
3. Don't hesitate to ask the taxi fleet about every detail when signing the rental contract.
4. Choosing the right taxi car is an important investment in the success of your business.
