## Advantages of renting a taxi car

In big cities taxi services are in high demand.

Each company that rents out cars may have its own set of terms. Pay attention to the following:

1. How much time a day the rental covers.

Some taxi services rent out a car for a few hours, others for a full day. A daily rental costs more. But the car is also available at any hour of the day or season.

2. Getting a waybill and passing the pre-trip medical check.

To go on the line you must have all the necessary documents. A traffic police (GIBDD) officer may check them, and if you don't have them you'll be fined. The car owner may do the pre-trip medical check at the taxi company or let the driver do it on their own.

3. Fuel, car washes, parking and fees.

These are the renter's costs.
