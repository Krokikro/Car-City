# Choosing a taxi car: from economy to comfort

![Choosing a taxi car: from economy to comfort](https://car-city.pro/themes/imgs/banners/8.jpg)

## Renting a taxi car: Comfort or Economy?

Starting in a taxi is always a tough choice, especially when it comes to the car. If you're new to renting, don't chase premium cars thinking they'll earn you more. In reality, your financial success depends on using your time well and keeping costs down.

## Economy vs. Comfort: what should a newcomer choose?

At first glance the "Economy" fare class may seem the most attractive, especially for new drivers. An inexpensive car and low rent seem like the perfect start. But don't forget other factors. Working in Economy often means driving less comfortable cars and long drives between orders.

Comfort and Comfort+ offer roomier, more modern cars. The rent is higher, but so is the level of service expected. Passengers expect a more comfortable ride, a car in excellent condition, attentive service and a smooth trip. It will be hard for a newcomer to meet these standards straight away if they're still getting the hang of the traffic rules and professional driving skills.

## How the brand affects earnings

Many people new to taxi work wrongly think that a prestigious car brand will bring higher earnings. In fact, the trip costs more for the passenger, but renting such a car costs much more too. The economics turn out worse than expected, and a premium car won't bring extra income.

What's more, a simple, reliable car affects how effective your work is overall. An inexpensive but reliable car lets you work longer, get into accidents less often and save on repairs. A comfortable interior and good sound insulation mean you get less tired, recover faster, work more productively and do more trips a day.

## What about Moscow and Saint Petersburg?

Moscow and Saint Petersburg are big cities with unique conditions. Renting a taxi car is more regulated there than in the regions. The minimum car class is a Solaris or a Rio, which guarantees more comfortable conditions both for you and for passengers. Remember that service quality and comfort are essential parts of success in a taxi.

## Air conditioning: a must or a luxury?

Air conditioning is an essential part of a pleasant trip in summer. Working without it is not only uncomfortable but also bad for your health. Overheating can lead to tiredness, poor concentration, wrong decisions and unpredictable consequences. Before signing the rental contract, check that the air conditioning works: switch it on and make sure it cools the interior.

## Manual vs. automatic: choosing the transmission

The choice between a manual and an automatic transmission depends on the driver's personal preferences and experience. A manual is more economical but needs certain skills and effort. An automatic is more convenient and easier, but uses more fuel. Decide which option is most convenient and profitable in your situation.

## Start small, but remember what matters

Your path in a taxi starts with choosing a car. Don't chase premium brands. Start by renting a budget option, but don't forget about comfort, safety and efficiency. The right rental car will help you earn more, cut costs and avoid unpleasant situations.
