# List of taxi cars 2026: full breakdown, requirements, list of models, comparison and market forecast

![List of taxi cars 2026](https://car-city.pro/themes/imgs/novosti/spisok-mashin-dlya-taksi-2026-polnyij-razbor-trebovaniya-spisok-modelej-sravnenie-i-prognoz-ryinka/img1.webp)

## Why the reform was introduced now

The taxi localisation law came as a response to several factors at once:

- harder deliveries of foreign cars and growing dependence on parallel imports;
- the wish to increase the share of Russian brands in mass passenger transport;
- the need to make fleets predictable, so that aggregators can count on the quality and uniformity of cars.

The current taxi fleet in big cities is badly worn, and renewal is uneven. The new law sets a single principle: only cars made in Russia or with a large share of localised parts will be able to work.

## Which cars are allowed to work

A car can work as a taxi if it:

- meets the localisation level requirements that also apply to government purchases
- is made under a special investment contract (SPIK) in Russia until 2033.

If one of these conditions is met, the model is included in the Ministry of Industry and Trade list. The document is based on the manufacturer's data, process sheets and supporting documents.

## How the Ministry of Industry and Trade list is made

The list is published on the ministry's official website and updated as new models appear, production lines launch or localisation expands.

Each model is listed with:

- the make,
- the model,
- the WMI code (the manufacturer code under the international classification),
- the localisation status (SPIK list or points system).

This lets taxi services and drivers know in advance which cars they can buy without risking a refusal at registration.

## List of taxi cars 2026: a catalogue with detailed notes

**Lada**

The Lada range is still the most popular choice for taxis. Granta and Iskra suit the Economy fare class, and Vesta offers a balanced level of comfort. Largus is in demand with delivery services and for trips with luggage. Niva Legend and Travel are on the list thanks to localisation, but their characteristics make them better suited to rural areas and regions with bad roads.

**УАЗ**

These models are rare in city taxis, but thanks to Russian production they fully meet the law's requirements. The Patriot is used by transport services in hard-to-reach areas. The Hunter is technically allowed, but its design limits passenger comfort.

**Sollers**

The SP7 was designed as an affordable car for corporate and government fleets. With the right equipment it can be used in the Economy class. Production is localised and spare parts are available, which matters with high mileage.

**Evolute**

The Evolute electric range is one of the growth areas of the Russian car industry. The compact I-PRO is best for short city routes, while the I-SKY and I-SPACE can be used in the Comfort class. The low cost per kilometre makes these models profitable for taxis where charging infrastructure is available.

**Voyah**

Premium electric cars of the "Comfort+" and "Business" classes. The Free is often chosen for executive trips, the Dream suits corporate clients thanks to its roomy interior, and the Passion is aimed at those who value performance and rich equipment.

**Москвич**

City crossovers and electric cars adapted to Russian conditions. Москвич 3 and 3e are already widely used by taxi fleets. The combination of an affordable price and economy makes these models potential bestsellers in 2026–2027. Москвич 6 and 8 suit fare classes with more comfort.

## Why some popular models are not on the list

Some cars made in Russia are missing because of:

- incomplete localisation of parts,
- confirmations under the points system not yet issued,
- production being in a transition stage.

For example, Haval Jolion is missing because not all localisation stages have been officially confirmed, although it is assembled at the Tula plant. The Ministry of Industry and Trade states clearly that the list will be expanded.

## What will happen to older foreign cars

Cars registered as taxis before 1 March 2026 will keep working until their permit expires (usually 5 years). So older cars will leave the market gradually, in 2027–2031.

This will smooth the transition for regions where the fleet is mostly made up of foreign cars.

The requirements are being introduced in stages:

- the Far East from 2030,
- Siberia and the Kaliningrad region from 2028,
- other regions from 2026.

This approach takes into account the availability of new models and the specifics of regional markets.

## Requirements for the car and the driver in 2026

The car must have:

- a colour scheme or the chequered taxi stripes,
- an orange roof light,
- a taximeter,
- information about the carrier and fares.

The driver must have:

- a medical certificate,
- at least 3 years of driving experience,
- no serious offences,
- no unpaid fines,
- self-employed or sole proprietor (IP) status,
- a contract with an ordering service.

## How trip prices will change

According to industry estimates, trip prices will start rising in 2026–2027 for these reasons:

- fleet renewal and buying new cars,
- fewer drivers (down to −20% according to forecasts),
- cars becoming temporarily less available,
- higher maintenance costs.

The rise will be especially noticeable in big cities, where the share of older foreign cars in the taxi fleet is high.

The switch to localised cars is a large-scale reform of the taxi market. It's happening gradually, but it will change the structure of fleets for years to come. In the coming years the list of changes will grow, and taxi fleets need to keep this in mind when planning fleet renewal.
