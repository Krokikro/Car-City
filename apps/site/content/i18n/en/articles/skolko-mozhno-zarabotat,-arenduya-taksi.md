# How much can you earn renting a taxi?

![How much can you earn renting a taxi?](https://car-city.pro/themes/imgs/banners/11.jpg)

Taxi work is becoming more and more popular: public transport isn't comfortable for everyone, and not everyone has their own car. This gives drivers a chance to earn extra or main income. In the past people mostly used their own car for taxi work, but today renting is preferred. At a rental company, newcomers get help choosing the best car model.

### How earnings work

Total income from a rented taxi depends on various factors. In big cities or in a small area with competition, minimum earnings are about 10 thousand rubles a day. Part of this goes on rent (roughly from a thousand rubles) and the taxi service's commission.

What matters a lot for a driver is mobility and how fast they get a passenger from one point to another. The key factor is time spent behind the wheel (the more, the higher the revenue). You can increase profit by driving at rush hours: average earnings reach 15 thousand a day.

Another important factor is the car class: Economy, Comfort, Comfort+, premium. The minimum earnings of a Comfort class driver are 15 thousand. For higher classes the earnings are higher too (passengers may leave tips from 500 rubles). A driver's profit depends on the area: in big cities like Saint Petersburg or Moscow it's several times higher than in small towns (from 2,5 to 5 thousand).

### Why renting a car pays off

Owners of personal cars usually take good care of them, while taxi work means frequent trips that increase wear. This leads to higher costs for maintenance and car washes and lower income over time. The driver starts turning down orders, avoiding trips out of town or on bad roads. As a result profit drops, and carrying passengers in your own car becomes uncomfortable because your personal space is invaded and the interior gets dirty fast.

### Advantages of working on a rented car:

- No need to spend money on licensing and extra equipment;
- The car owner takes care of maintenance;
- It's psychologically easier to use a rented car;
- No need to buy an insurance policy or go through technical inspections.

It's a great option for newcomers: you can start working right away, without extra worries. CarCity has discounts and special offers for long-term rental.
