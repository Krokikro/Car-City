# JAC Motors – a success story

![JAC Motors – a success story](https://car-city.pro/themes/imgs/banners/10.jpg)

At the start, JAC Motors specialised in making engines and car parts, but in 1969 it developed and presented its own truck. Later it started making buses and vans, and in 2002, with support from Hyundai, the company began producing its first passenger cars.

### Constant development

In the 2010s JAC Motors greatly expanded its range, offering customers many models, including compact city sedans, crossovers and pickups. One of the main reasons for JAC Motors' success was its drive for innovation and constant improvement of production technology. JAC Motors invested heavily in research and development, which allowed it to build cars that meet high quality and safety standards. Thanks to strategic partnerships and collaborations with leading global carmakers, the company was able to launch modern, competitive models.

One of the important milestones in JAC Motors' history was its work on clean transport solutions. In recent years the company has focused on making electric cars, adapting its technology to the needs of the times and caring for the environment. JAC cars have proven themselves as reliable and economical vehicles, which has helped attract new customers.

### Today

Today JAC Motors keeps growing fast, strengthening its position both in China and on international markets. Its focus on customers and flexible production let the company react quickly to market changes and meet growing demand. Innovative development, environmental responsibility and high product quality continue to earn JAC Motors recognition and trust among car owners around the world.
