# Up-to-date tips for drivers planning to rent a car for taxi work

![Up-to-date tips for drivers](https://car-city.pro/themes/imgs/banners/12.jpg)

## Tips for drivers renting a car for taxi work

If you are about to start working and earning in a taxi on a rented car, here are a few tips:

- Before signing, read the contract of the company renting out the taxi carefully.
- Plan your shifts in advance and don't get distracted by personal errands while working. To save on fuel, consider renting a car long-term.
- Choose busy areas with lots of orders: there are not enough taxis there, so prices go up. Traffic is high on weekday mornings, when many people rush to work, and in the evening, when they head home. At night there are no traffic jams and you can take several orders; these usually come on weekends in the city centre.
- Paying the rent several days in advance can be a better deal.
- Follow the traffic rules to avoid traffic police (GIBDD) fines and cut your costs.
- You can even save on fuel if you choose a more economical car and take orders along your way. Sometimes it pays more to wait for an order in the centre than to drive out to the outskirts.
- Remember that to work successfully in a taxi on a rented car you also need to keep the car in good technical condition. Check the oil, brake fluid, antifreeze and other fluids regularly, as well as the tyres and lights. This helps you avoid accidents and expensive repairs.
- Also, don't forget your personal safety. Before your shift, make sure your phone is charged enough to stay in touch with the dispatcher and passengers at all times. Try to avoid dangerous areas and stay in control of the situation during trips.
- And remember that building trust with passengers matters. Politeness, professionalism and good communication will help you get good reviews and repeat orders, which in the end affects your earnings. Good luck in your new job, and may every trip bring you only positive experiences and satisfaction from your work!
