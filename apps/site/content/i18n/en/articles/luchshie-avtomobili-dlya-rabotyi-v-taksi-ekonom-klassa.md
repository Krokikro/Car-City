## Leading Economy class cars for taxi rental

Each car on the list has its own advantages that suit different working conditions and taxi drivers' preferences.

## KIA RIO

A popular sedan with a 1,4 l engine and fuel consumption of about 6 l/100 km. It's economical and reliable, which makes it a favourite with taxi drivers despite its slightly stiff suspension.

## Hyundai Solaris

A large 480 l boot and economical fuel consumption. The rear suspension is stable, and spare parts are cheap.

The Volkswagen Polo Liftback is ideal for taxi work thanks to its wide boot and roomy interior. Its size makes parking easy, and its ground clearance protects it from damage.

The Volkswagen Polo stands out for easy access to the boot and good agility in the city, which makes it a good choice for narrow streets. The stabiliser links need attention, which is worth keeping in mind when choosing a car.

## Renault Logan

It offers good warranties and reliability, which helps you save on maintenance. The car's compact size makes it easy to move between cars and find a parking space even in very busy areas. In heavy traffic the Logan shows great handling and stability.

## LADA Vesta CNG

It attracts customers with its low cost and the ability to make long trips without frequent refuelling.

This car is the best choice for a taxi thanks to fuel costs half as high as with petrol. The gas system switches to gas automatically after warming up, and when the gas runs out, the engine switches to petrol. With both petrol and methane on board you can drive up to 1000 km without refuelling, which is handy for long trips. Power drops slightly from 106 to 96 hp, but the car stays lively. Taxi drivers praise the comfortable suspension and attractive design.

Keep in mind that because of the gas equipment, the usable boot space has shrunk to 250 l, which can be inconvenient when carrying large luggage.

The choice of model depends on the driver's needs and working conditions.
