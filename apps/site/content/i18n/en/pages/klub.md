# Car City Private Club

The Private Club is the Car City drivers' community. It is open to everyone who has taken a car on rent-to-own. Here people don't just work — they connect, go to events and are the first to hear about new cars in the fleet.

## What club members get

- Invitations to celebrations, competitions and presentations of new cars
- Contests and prize draws with cash prizes
- Networking with drivers who work with us and are buying out their cars
- Fleet news and new models before anyone else

## How to join the club

Take a car on rent-to-own: club membership is part of the rent-to-own terms. Leave a request, and a manager will explain how it works and pick a car that fits your schedule.
