### Which Chery models are good for taxi work?

For working on the line we recommend the Chery Tiggo 4 and Tiggo 7 Pro. These are crossovers with good handling, enough ground clearance, a roomy interior and nice features: air conditioning, multimedia, a phone charger. They are fuel-efficient and easily qualify for the "Comfort" class with most aggregators.

### Do I need to pay a deposit?

No, Car City does not require a deposit for car rental. We give you cars without frozen funds or deposits — you only pay rent on the schedule you choose. Handy if you are just starting out and don't want to spend a large sum right away.

### What documents do I need to rent?

To rent a Chery for taxi work you need only the basics: passport, driver's licence and proof that you are not a beginner behind the wheel — 3 or more years of experience. We take care of everything else. Paperwork takes very little time, and you can start working on the line the same day.

### Can I connect to Yandex Taxi through you?

Yes, we help with registration and preparing for the Yandex Taxi exam. If you have not worked with an aggregator before, we'll explain how everything works before you connect. Experienced drivers get fast-track paperwork and priority on requests.

### What should I do if the car breaks down or I have an accident?

You can contact our support team at any time — it works around the clock. We'll help with towing, a replacement car, accident paperwork and related issues.
