## Frequently asked questions

### What documents do I need to start renting?

Passport, driver's licence, sole proprietor (IP) registration certificate, taxi permit (entered in KIS "ART"), bank card. If you want to consider rent-to-own, you'll also need to sign a separate contract.

### Can I choose the car myself?

Yes, you can come to the office and look at the available cars. You can also agree on a selection by phone — we'll offer several suitable models for the Economy, Comfort and Comfort+ tariffs.

### What does maintenance include?

We provide full technical support for the car for the whole rental period. It includes regular maintenance, oil and filter changes, suspension diagnostics, and checks of the brakes, battery and other parts. The car is handed over clean and in good working order.

### Are there any experience or age restrictions?

To rent — 3 or more years of experience. For rent-to-own, individual terms are possible if the car is for personal use. This is discussed at a meeting with the manager.

### How does the holiday work on rent-to-own?

Every six months you can take a break — 7 calendar days with no rent-to-own charges. The car stays with you, the contract stays in force, and the payment schedule shifts one week forward.

### Where can I pick up the car?

All cars are handed over at the head office on Udaltsova st. (Moscow). You can pick up the car in person after signing the contract and having your documents checked.
