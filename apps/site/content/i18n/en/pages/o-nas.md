# About us

Car City is a convenient car rental service for taxi drivers. We have been working for more than 7 years, and all this time we keep improving our service so that renting is easy for you and as profitable as possible.

On top of that, we offer our drivers a unique option: a rent-to-own contract, so over time you can become the owner of your own car.

![about-intro](https://car-city.pro/themes/img/about/new/about-intro.webp)

## Our advantages

- A large choice of cars in different classes
- Regular discounts for new and current drivers
- 8 days off after six months of work
- No requirements for the number of orders or working hours
- No accumulating deposit
- Contests and events with cash prizes

## Why is it worth working with us?

![](https://car-city.pro/themes/img/about/new/why-vigoda.webp)

Working with Car City is not only easy but also profitable. Right now our fleet has more than 600 cars of different makes and classes.

Before being rented out, every car gets full maintenance and cleaning, so you don't have to worry about repairs or other hassles. Just take the car, drive and earn!

We want our drivers to choose their own schedule freely and work when it suits them. When you sign the contract, you can choose from several payment schedules.

Send a request, join our community of drivers, and see for yourself!
