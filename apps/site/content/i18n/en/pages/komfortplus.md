## Advantages of Comfort+ class cars

Comfort+ is one of the three classes you can choose when ordering a taxi, along with Comfort and Economy. The cars we rent out have a presentable, roomy and comfortable interior. Passengers can relax in these cars even on a long trip. That's why Comfort+ is popular not only with well-off customers who value their status, but also with passengers planning long-distance trips.

Comfort+ class cars have special features. For example, the interiors have USB ports for charging. Climate control keeps the perfect temperature whatever the weather outside. Passengers can get on with their business or rest on the way, and that's worth paying for Comfort+.

Some aggregators' rules cover not only the car's specs but also the service. Sometimes they require water or other amenities for passengers.

## Questions and answers

### Which cars belong to the "Comfort+" class?

Modern, stylish and comfortable cars where you feel confident both behind the wheel and in the passenger seat. A roomy interior and a smooth ride — everything is made for comfortable trips in the city and beyond.

### What do I need to rent a "Comfort+" taxi car?

You must be at least 21 and have 3 or more years of driving experience. You'll also need a passport, a driver's licence and SNILS. We don't like red tape, so we've made the paperwork quick.

### What is included in the car rental price in Moscow?

The base price includes everything you need to drive with confidence: insurance, maintenance and round-the-clock support. You just take the keys and go. We make sure the cars are clean, fuelled and in perfect order.

### What are the payment terms?

Payment is made in advance — for the whole period at once or day by day, depending on the tariff. If you get a fine, we'll let you know, and then you'll need to pay it by the due date.

### What payment methods are available?

We accept everything: bank cards, transfers, cash. You can also pay by bank transfer as a legal entity if you are taking the car for business. We'll prepare invoices and contracts ourselves. Rent payments are charged every day at 12:00 in the "Элемент для водителя" (Element for drivers) app
