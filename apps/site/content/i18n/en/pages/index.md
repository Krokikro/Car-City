## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start working with Car City? Driver requirements

To rent a taxi car in Moscow from Car City, you just need to meet a few simple criteria. We don't set excessive requirements, but we do care about ride safety.

**Citizenship**

To drive our cars, you need citizenship of one of these countries: Russia, Belarus, Kyrgyzstan, Kazakhstan, South Ossetia, Abkhazia

**Experience**

To rent a car or take it rent-to-own, you just need a driver's licence held for 3 years or more

**Age**

The minimum age to start working with Car City is 21

**Documents**

Driver's licence, passport, KIS "ART" and a certificate of no criminal record. With the full set of documents you can take a car for taxi work right after signing up.

### Will I get a car if other fleets gave me negative reviews?

We do look at reviews from other fleets during the check, but the final decision is made by the manager after a personal interview.

### What is the fleet commission?

Our fleet commission is 4%. That's one of the most driver-friendly offers among taxi car rental fleets.

### What is the minimum rental period?

The minimum rental period for any of our cars is 30 days

### Can I work with another taxi fleet?

With a standard taxi rental in Moscow — no, the car is assigned to Car City. But if you plan to buy the vehicle, there are no restrictions on choosing an aggregator.

### Are there any bonuses for current drivers?

Yes, of course. With our "Bring a friend" promotion you get 5 thousand rubles for every new driver who signs a rental contract with us, and 15 thousand rubles for a rent-to-own contract.

### Where can I rent a taxi car?

You can rent a taxi car in Moscow with no middlemen or hidden fees at the Car City taxi fleet. Our offices and taxi rental parking lots are at three addresses:

- metro Mitino (15 bldg. 3, 1-y Mitinsky lane);

- metro Michurinsky Prospekt (36 Udaltsova st.).

If you're looking for a taxi rental with a licence and no deposit, you don't have to drive all over Moscow. Come to the nearest Car City office with your documents to rent a taxi car.
