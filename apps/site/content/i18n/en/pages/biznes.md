## Questions and answers

### Can I simply rent a business class car, without buying it out?

Business class cars are currently offered only on a rent-to-own basis. If you need a regular rental, have a look at the Comfort and Comfort+ tariffs.

### What are the driver requirements for business class?

A passport, a driving licence, KIS "ART" and a certificate of no criminal record. The app may ask for an extra check before you can work in the business tariff — we will help you prepare.

### How much do I need to pay upfront?

It depends on the model: the Hongqi H5 has no down payment, while the Voyah Dream and GAC M8 need 300,000 ₽. A manager will work out the exact figures for your schedule.
