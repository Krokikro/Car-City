## Questions and answers

### Which cars belong to the "Business" class?

Spacious, comfortable premium-level cars: an easy seating position, a well-designed cabin and a smooth ride for trips in the city and beyond.

### What do I need to rent a car for "Business" taxi?

You must be at least 21 years old with at least 3 years of driving experience. You'll also need a passport, a driver's licence and SNILS. We don't like red tape, so we've made the paperwork quick.

### What is included in the car rental price in Moscow?

The base price includes everything you need to drive with confidence: insurance, servicing and round-the-clock support. You just take the keys and go. We make sure the cars are clean, fuelled and in perfect order.

### What are the payment terms?

Payment is made in advance — for the whole term at once or day by day, depending on the tariff. If you get a fine, we'll notify you, and you'll then need to pay it within the set deadline.

### Which payment methods are available?

We accept everything: bank cards, transfers and cash. You can also pay by bank transfer as a legal entity if you're taking the car for business. We'll prepare the invoices and contracts ourselves. Rental payments are charged daily at 12:00 in the "Element for drivers" app

### Can I take a business class car on rent-to-own?

Yes. All three models are available both for rent and for rent-to-own over 1–3 years. The Hongqi H5 is available with no down payment, while the Voyah Dream and GAC M8 need a 300 000 ₽ down payment. A manager will make an exact calculation for your schedule.
