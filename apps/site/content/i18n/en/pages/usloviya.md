# Taxi car rental terms

### Citizenship

To drive our cars, you need citizenship of one of these countries: Russia, Belarus, Kyrgyzstan, Kazakhstan, South Ossetia, Abkhazia

### Driving experience

To rent a car or take it rent-to-own, you just need a driver's licence held for 3 years or more

### Age

The minimum age to start working with Car City is 21

### Documents

Driver's licence, passport, KIS "ART" and a certificate of no criminal record

### Self-employed or sole proprietor

The driver needs to register as self-employed or as a sole proprietor (IP)
