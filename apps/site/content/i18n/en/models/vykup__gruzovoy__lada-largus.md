## About the Lada Largus

The Lada Largus van, with a 1,6 L petrol engine (90–106 hp) and a five-speed manual gearbox, looks functional and reliable – ideal for city logistics and small business. Its compact dimensions (length — 4 470 mm, height — 1 670 mm) and a turning radius of just 5,5 m give it excellent manoeuvrability in tight courtyards and narrow streets.

Under the bonnet is the proven VAZ-21129 petrol engine producing 106 hp (78 kW) at 5 800 rpm, with maximum torque of 148 N·m at 4 200 rpm. A modern multipoint fuel injection system gives economical consumption and stable performance in any conditions. The vehicle meets the Euro-5 emission standard, and a well-designed engine heating system guarantees confident starts in cold weather.
