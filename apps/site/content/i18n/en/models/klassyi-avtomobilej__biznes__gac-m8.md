## About the Gac M8

The GAC M8's look emphasises its premium character and focus on comfortable passenger transport. A large body, a massive grille, expressive lights and a spacious cabin make the car suitable for high-class taxi work, transfers, business trips and carrying families. The three-row layout comfortably seats up to seven passengers, and separate second-row seats give extra comfort on long trips.

The GAC M8 combines a smooth ride, good dynamics and a high level of equipment. A powerful petrol engine, automatic gearbox and comfort-tuned suspension let you feel confident both in heavy city traffic and on the motorway.
