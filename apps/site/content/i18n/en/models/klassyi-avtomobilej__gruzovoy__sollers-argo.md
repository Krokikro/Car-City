## About the Sollers Argo

The Sollers Argo, with a 2.0 L diesel engine (130 hp) and a six-speed manual gearbox, is clearly designed for city logistics. Its modest dimensions (length 5 300 mm, height 2 650 mm), low loading height (785 mm) and 11,5 m turning radius make it exceptionally manoeuvrable even in tight city conditions.

Under the bonnet is the HFC4DB2-1D turbodiesel producing 130 hp (96 kW) at 3 600 rpm, with maximum torque of 285 N·m at 2 200–2 600 rpm, Common Rail technology, the Euro-5 emission standard and liquid fuel heating for reliable starts in cold weather.

The cargo area of the standard version has internal dimensions of 3 120 × 1 720 × 1 720 mm and a volume of up to 9 m³, fitting up to 4 euro pallets. Gross vehicle weight is from 2,5 to 3,5 t, with dual rear wheels for reliability and stability.
