## About the Geely Emgrand

The Geely Emgrand is an example of a compact sedan that harmoniously combines good looks with innovative design touches.

The cabin has a clean, strict style that catches the eye straight away. Two-tone trim, seats with perforated eco-leather inserts and large multimedia screens create a cosy atmosphere, as if you were at home.

### Rent a Geely Emgrand in comfort

The interior is pleasantly ergonomic and spacious: the cabin is 1,5 metres wide and 0,97 m high.

The large boot is ideal for long trips and holds up to 500 litres with the rear seats folded.

### Renting a Geely Emgrand taxi is reliable

The Geely Emgrand's engine and transmission are time-tested. Their main advantages are high reliability, low maintenance demands and economical fuel consumption.

An original detail is the wide LED light bar at the rear, which gives the car a premium look. The delayed headlight switch-off feature lets you safely reach a lit area in poor visibility.

Safety was a major focus when the model was developed. The body is made of high-strength steel, which gives it high rigidity.
