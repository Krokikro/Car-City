## About the Sollers SF4 L4

With its refrigerated body, the Sollers SF4 L4 is clearly practical and built for professional transport. The long L4 wheelbase carries a roomy insulated box while keeping the vehicle easy to use in the city. It suits deliveries of food, frozen goods, ready meals, flowers, medicines and other cargo that needs a set temperature.
