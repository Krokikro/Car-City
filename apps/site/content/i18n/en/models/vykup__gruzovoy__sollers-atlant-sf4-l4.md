## About the Sollers SF4 L4

With its refrigerated body, the Sollers SF4 L4 is clearly practical and built for professional transport. The long L4 wheelbase carries a roomy insulated box while keeping the vehicle easy to use in the city. It suits deliveries of food, frozen goods, ready meals, flowers, medicines and other cargo that needs a set temperature.

Under the bonnet is a 2,7 L diesel engine producing 150 hp with maximum torque of 355 N·m, paired with a six-speed manual gearbox. Rear-wheel drive and high torque keep the vehicle working confidently even when fully loaded. The refrigeration unit keeps the required temperature in the cargo area, and the larger L4 body makes the vehicle efficient for regular city and intercity transport.
