## About the Voyah Dream

The Voyah Dream's look emphasises its premium status and focus on comfortable passenger transport. A large body, expressive lights and a spacious cabin make the car especially suitable for high-class taxi work, transfers, trips with families and passengers who value extra comfort. The three-row layout comfortably seats up to seven people, and separate second-row seats add comfort on long trips.

The Voyah Dream combines dynamics, a smooth ride and a high level of equipment. The hybrid powertrain and all-wheel drive give confident driving both in the city and on the motorway, while the spacious cabin, effective sound insulation, climate system and comfortable passenger seats make the car convenient for long hours on the line. Thanks to its premium equipment and capacity, the Dream is well suited to orders where comfort, a presentable car and the level of service matter most.
