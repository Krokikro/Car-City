Advantages of Economy class cars

A lot of people use taxis, and most of them choose the "Economy" class. They like the low fare combined with fast pickup. Statistics show that "Economy" is often used for short trips, while for longer distances customers more often order "Comfort", since cars in that class are more comfortable. So by renting an Economy class car you can serve many customers, earning through volume and possibly through tips, which depend on how attentive you are to your passengers.

03

Key features of Economy class cars:

  • Practicality. These models usually have a compact interior and a practical trunk for small luggage. Passenger seats are usually not adjustable, but that's no problem on short trips.
  • Easy maintenance. There aren't many smart assistance systems on board.
  • Price. "Economy" is the cheapest class of models in our fleet.
  • Popular with customers. Rides are cheap, so many people are happy to pay a taxi driver to get where they're going faster.

FAQ

Questions and answers

What is included in the price of renting a car for Economy taxi?

The price already includes all the basics — maintenance, insurance, a taxi licence (if required). We also take care of oil changes, tyre fitting and washing. You just pay the rent, and we handle the rest.

What do I need to rent a taxi car in Moscow?

You don't need anything special to rent an Economy class car. The main thing is that you are at least 21 and have at least 3 years of driving experience. Documents: passport and driver's licence — all standard.

What should I do if the car breaks down?

Nobody is safe from breakdowns, so if something happens to the car, call us.

Your Economy class car rental contract lists the phone number of our support service, which works every day. We'll try to fix the problem as quickly as possible: we'll send a tow truck or roadside help and, if needed, replace the car with one that works so you can keep working.

The main thing is not to repair the car yourself, especially without our approval.
