<h2 id="who-can-rent-a-car-for-taxi-work">Who can rent a car for taxi work</h2><p>Requirements for the renter:</p>
<ul class="ticks">
<li>Citizenship and documents. Citizens of Russia, Belarus, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan and South Ossetia can rent a car. Citizens of other countries can rent a car only if they have a residence permit (VNZh) and are registered as a sole proprietor (IP).</li>
<li>Age. &quot;The minimum driver age is 21&quot;</li>
<li>Driving experience. &quot;As a rule, at least 3 years of driving experience is required&quot;</li>
</ul>
<p>Extra requirements (mainly from the aggregators):</p>
<ul class="ticks">
<li>Appearance. The driver needs to dress in a business style and look neat, especially when working in the Business class.</li>
<li>Communicating with passengers. Politeness, tidiness, knowing Russian and being able to use a navigation app are also required, especially in premium categories.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="main-documents-for-renting-a-car">Main documents for renting a car</h2><h3>Car rental contract</h3>
<p>The main legal document that gives the driver the right to use the car temporarily. It must include:</p>
<ul class="tiles">
<li>Full names of the parties (full name and passport details, or company details)</li>
<li>The subject of the contract (make, model, VIN and licence plate of the car)</li>
<li>The rental term and the terms for extending or ending it</li>
<li>The rental fee and how it is paid</li>
<li>The parties&#39; liability and penalties</li>
<li>Signatures of both parties</li>
</ul>
<h3>Car handover certificate</h3>
<p>The certificate is drawn up on the day the car is handed over and contains:</p>
<ul class="tiles">
<li>A detailed description of the car&#39;s condition</li>
<li>Fuel level and mileage at handover</li>
<li>Notes on any external damage</li>
<li>The date and signatures of the parties</li>
</ul>
<h3>KIS &quot;ART&quot;</h3>
<p>&quot;The driver must be registered in KIS &quot;ART&quot; (the Integrated Information System of Taxi Market Aggregators)&quot;</p>
<h3>Self-employed or IP</h3>
<p>If you plan to work directly, you need to register in one of these forms:</p>
<ul class="ticks">
<li>Self-employed (SMZ). The simplest way to earn officially.</li>
<li>Sole proprietor (IP). Useful if you want to work with several taxi fleets.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="step-by-step-guide-to-renting">Step-by-step guide to renting</h2><h3>Checking the outside of the car</h3>
<p>Before signing the documents, the renter should inspect the car carefully:</p>
<ul class="tiles">
<li>Check the body for scratches, dents and chips</li>
<li>Make sure the headlights, windows, mirrors and wipers work</li>
<li>Check the interior, the boot and the tyres</li>
<li>Record all defects you find on photo/video</li>
</ul>
<h3>Signing the rental contract</h3>
<p>After the inspection you sign the rental contract, which sets out:</p>
<ul class="tiles">
<li>The car model and the rental term</li>
<li>The price and payment schedule</li>
<li>Liability for accidents and breakdowns</li>
<li>The option to extend the rental</li>
</ul>
<h3>Handing over the car</h3>
<p>After the contract is signed:</p>
<ul class="tiles">
<li>The parties sign the handover certificate</li>
<li>The driver gets the keys, the vehicle registration certificate (STS), the OSAGO policy and the waybill</li>
<li>The car is officially considered rented out</li>
</ul>
<h3>Working and returning the car</h3>
<p>&quot;The minimum rental term is 14 days, but it can be extended for any period&quot;</p>
<p>If the driver plans to end the rental:</p>
<ul class="ticks">
<li>Notify the manager 3 days before the planned return date</li>
<li>When you return the car, there&#39;s a final inspection and a return certificate is signed</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="important-rules-and-responsibility">Important rules and responsibility</h2><p>The driver must take good care of the car:</p>
<ul class="tiles">
<li>Keep an eye on its technical condition and report any faults</li>
<li>Not hand the car over to third parties without the owner&#39;s consent</li>
<li>Fill up with good quality fuel</li>
<li>Keep the body and interior clean</li>
</ul>
<p>If there&#39;s an accident:</p>
<ol class="stepl">
<li>The driver must report it to the insurance company and to the car owner</li>
<li>Record all the details at the scene (photos, diagrams, statements from both sides)</li>
<li>Call the traffic police (GIBDD)</li>
</ol>
<p>Apart from that, the driver should avoid these violations:</p>
<ul class="tiles">
<li>An expired diagnostic card</li>
<li>Wrong parking</li>
<li>Breaking the terms of work with an aggregator (Yandex, Citymobil)</li>
<li>Working without a valid licence</li>
</ul>

<p>Car City offers help at every stage:</p>
<ul class="tiles">
<li>Getting the documents you need to connect to aggregators (KIS &quot;ART&quot;, certification, licence)</li>
<li>Advice on the contract and rental terms</li>
<li>Help with resolving disputes and restoring access to aggregators</li>
</ul>
