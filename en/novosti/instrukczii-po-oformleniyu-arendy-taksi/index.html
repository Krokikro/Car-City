How to rent a taxi car: step-by-step paperwork guide

Contents

  1. Who can rent a car for taxi work

  3. Step-by-step guide to renting
  4. Important rules and responsibility
  5. Conclusion

Find out how to sign a taxi car rental contract the right way. Useful tips, the documents you need and important details.

Who can rent a car for taxi work

Requirements for the renter:

  • Citizenship and documents. Citizens of Russia, Belarus, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan and South Ossetia can rent a car. Citizens of other countries can rent a car only if they have a residence permit (VNZh) and are registered as a sole proprietor (IP).
  • Age. "The minimum driver age is 21"
  • Driving experience. "As a rule, at least 3 years of driving experience is required"

Extra requirements (mainly from the aggregators):

  • Appearance. The driver needs to dress in a business style and look neat, especially when working in the Business class.
  • Communicating with passengers. Politeness, tidiness, knowing Russian and being able to use a navigation app are also required, especially in premium categories.

Main documents for renting a car

Car rental contract

The main legal document that gives the driver the right to use the car temporarily. It must include:

  • Full names of the parties (full name and passport details, or company details)
  • The subject of the contract (make, model, VIN and licence plate of the car)
  • The rental term and the terms for extending or ending it
  • The rental fee and how it is paid
  • The parties' liability and penalties
  • Signatures of both parties

Car handover certificate

The certificate is drawn up on the day the car is handed over and contains:

  • A detailed description of the car's condition
  • Fuel level and mileage at handover
  • Notes on any external damage
  • The date and signatures of the parties

KIS "ART"

"The driver must be registered in KIS "ART" (the Integrated Information System of Taxi Market Aggregators)"

Self-employed or IP

If you plan to work directly, you need to register in one of these forms:

  • Self-employed (SMZ). The simplest way to earn officially.
  • Sole proprietor (IP). Useful if you want to work with several taxi fleets.

Step-by-step guide to renting

Checking the outside of the car

Before signing the documents, the renter should inspect the car carefully:

  • Check the body for scratches, dents and chips
  • Make sure the headlights, windows, mirrors and wipers work
  • Check the interior, the boot and the tyres
  • Record all defects you find on photo/video

Signing the rental contract

After the inspection you sign the rental contract, which sets out:

  • The car model and the rental term
  • The price and payment schedule
  • Liability for accidents and breakdowns
  • The option to extend the rental

Handing over the car

After the contract is signed:

  • The parties sign the handover certificate
  • The driver gets the keys, the vehicle registration certificate (STS), the OSAGO policy and the waybill
  • The car is officially considered rented out

Working and returning the car

"The minimum rental term is 14 days, but it can be extended for any period"

If the driver plans to end the rental:

  • Notify the manager 3 days before the planned return date
  • When you return the car, there's a final inspection and a return certificate is signed

Important rules and responsibility

The driver must take good care of the car:

  • Keep an eye on its technical condition and report any faults
  • Not hand the car over to third parties without the owner's consent
  • Fill up with good quality fuel
  • Keep the body and interior clean

If there's an accident:

  1. The driver must report it to the insurance company and to the car owner
  2. Record all the details at the scene (photos, diagrams, statements from both sides)
  3. Call the traffic police (GIBDD)

Apart from that, the driver should avoid these violations:

  • An expired diagnostic card
  • Wrong parking
  • Breaking the terms of work with an aggregator (Yandex, Citymobil)
  • Working without a valid licence
