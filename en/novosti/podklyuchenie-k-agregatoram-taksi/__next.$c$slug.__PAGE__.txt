<p>Taxi work is one of the most accessible ways to earn money in a big city. To carry passengers commercially, effectively and legally, drivers usually connect to aggregators: online services that act as go-betweens for the passenger and the taxi driver.</p>
<p>Today almost all work in the taxi segment depends on these platforms. Let&#39;s look at who can connect to aggregators, what documents you&#39;ll need, how the connection process works and what to pay attention to while working.</p>
<h2 id="advantages-of-connecting-to-taxi-aggregators">Advantages of connecting to taxi aggregators</h2><p>Services such as Yandex Pro, Citymobil, Indrive and others act as go-betweens for the passenger and the driver, taking care of orders, payment, navigation and even some of the legal side. Advantages of working through aggregators:</p>
<ul class="ticks">
<li>A constant flow of orders. They give drivers a steady stream of passengers thanks to their large audience and marketing.</li>
<li>A convenient payment system. They work with cashless payments: money for trips goes to a card or account, and receipts for cash are issued when needed. This makes bookkeeping and tax reporting easier.</li>
<li>Built-in navigation. The apps include built-in maps, traffic information, route optimisation and voice assistant support.</li>
<li>Rating and bonuses. The higher the driver&#39;s rating, the more orders and the higher the income. Good service brings bonuses, higher fares and priority for orders.</li>
<li>Support and training. The services offer round-the-clock technical support, and Yandex also offers training with an exam. This helps you avoid mistakes and get used to the work faster.</li>
<li>A flexible way of working. The driver can work on their own car or a rented one, or connect through a partner taxi fleet.</li>
<li>Legal work. This kind of work helps the driver stay within the law.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="requirements-for-connecting-to-taxi-aggregators">Requirements for connecting to taxi aggregators</h2><p>First of all, you need to meet the basic requirements. They apply both to the driver and to the car. Some are mandatory, others are more like recommendations, but all of them affect how well you connect and get orders.</p>
<h3>Documents you need</h3>
<p>To connect to an aggregator you&#39;ll need a standard set of documents:</p>
<ul class="ticks">
<li>Passport.</li>
<li>A category &quot;B&quot; driver&#39;s licence with at least 3 years of experience.</li>
<li>STS (vehicle registration certificate) for working on your own car.</li>
<li>A permit to carry passengers (licence). Mandatory for taxi work.</li>
<li>A tax number (INN), sole proprietor (IP) registration or self-employed status, depending on the aggregator.</li>
<li>A certificate of no criminal record. Required for official registration of the driver in the system.</li>
<li>KIS &quot;ART&quot;. Registration in the &quot;Taxi Order Control and Execution&quot; system (mandatory for legal work in Moscow and the Moscow region).</li>
<li>Passed certification. An exam on service standards (required, for example, when connecting to Yandex Pro through a taxi fleet or directly).</li>
</ul>
<h3>Car requirements</h3>
<p>The car must meet technical and appearance requirements:</p>
<ul class="tiles">
<li>Good technical condition. No faults, maintenance done on time.</li>
<li>A clean interior and body. A must, especially for the &quot;Comfort&quot; fare class and above.</li>
<li>Climate control or air conditioning. An important requirement, especially in hot weather.</li>
<li>Car age. As a rule, no older than 5–7 years for &quot;Comfort&quot; and above (there may be some leeway for &quot;Economy&quot;).</li>
<li>Body colour. Depends on the city&#39;s rules and the specific service.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Mobile device and internet</h3>
<p>Although aggregators don&#39;t set strict requirements for the smartphone, there are some recommendations:</p>
<ul class="tiles">
<li>Android OS. The apps run more reliably than on iOS.</li>
<li>GLONASS and GPS. Required for correct order tracking.</li>
<li>A reliable internet connection. Preferably unlimited 4G mobile internet.</li>
<li>Extra equipment. A phone holder, a charger, a thermal bag (for delivery), a dashcam.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="how-to-connect-to-aggregators">How to connect to aggregators</h2><p>You can connect to taxi aggregators in several ways: directly through the service&#39;s website, through a partner taxi fleet or through an intermediary. Overall the procedure is simple and may differ only in details.</p>
<h3>Registration and application</h3>
<p>The first step is registration:</p>
<ul class="ticks">
<li>On the aggregator&#39;s official website. You need to enter your full name and contact details, upload documents and fill in a form.</li>
<li>Through a partner taxi fleet. A popular way, especially if the driver doesn&#39;t have their own car or doesn&#39;t want to deal with the documents themselves.</li>
<li>Through an intermediary. A third-party service that helps you register quickly and start working, often for a percentage of orders.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Certification and exam</h3>
<p>Some aggregators (especially Yandex Pro) require certification: a short online test on service standards, rules for talking to passengers and behaviour in conflict situations.</p>
<p>You may also need a video interview, an appearance check, and photos of the car&#39;s interior and body.</p>
<p>Certification is done once and usually takes 15–30 minutes.</p>
<h3>Connecting through a taxi fleet</h3>
<p>If the driver doesn&#39;t have their own car or doesn&#39;t want to deal with paperwork, connecting through a taxi fleet is a great option:</p>
<ul class="tiles">
<li>Taxi fleets are already registered with aggregators and have licensed cars.</li>
<li>They take on most of the paperwork: registration in KIS &quot;ART&quot;, checking documents, handing over the car and making sure rules are followed.</li>
<li>All the driver has to do is pass the certification and start taking orders.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Confirmation and starting work</h3>
<p>After all the steps are done, the driver gets access to a work account in the aggregator&#39;s app, links a bank card for payouts and can choose the fare classes to work in (Economy, Comfort, Comfort+, Delivery, etc.).</p>
<p>If needed, the driver gets a briefing or help from the fleet&#39;s manager.</p>
<h2 id="working-with-aggregators-after-connecting">Working with aggregators after connecting</h2><p>After connecting to an aggregator, the driver gets access to the system through a mobile app. It shows orders, the route, the trip price and other information. Orders can be accepted manually or automatically, depending on the settings.</p>
<p>Payment can be cashless (a transfer to the driver&#39;s or the taxi fleet&#39;s account), cash (the passenger pays directly) or mixed. How often you get paid depends on the terms of the aggregator or partner.</p>
<p>The quality of work is rated by passengers and the system. Keep a businesslike manner, keep the car clean, don&#39;t be late and complete orders according to the service&#39;s rules. Repeated violations can lead to penalties, up to an account block.</p>
<h2 id="tips-and-recommendations">Tips and recommendations</h2><p>To work successfully with an aggregator, it&#39;s important not only to complete orders but also to raise your rating. It affects the number and quality of offers: the higher the rating, the better the trips.</p>
<p>Useful tips:</p>
<ul class="ticks">
<li>Follow service standards. A clean car, polite talk and careful driving are the basis of good ratings.</li>
<li>Don&#39;t break the aggregator&#39;s rules. Declining orders, frequent cancellations and passenger complaints can lower your rating or limit your access.</li>
<li>Keep track of your documents. Renew your diagnostic card and transport permit on time, and go through re-certification if required.</li>
<li>Use a reliable device. How well the app works depends on your smartphone. Android with GLONASS and a good connection is preferable.</li>
</ul>
<p>It&#39;s also important to remember to obey the law, and the traffic rules in particular.</p>
<h2 id="conclusion">Conclusion</h2><p>Connecting to taxi aggregators is a must for anyone who wants to work officially and steadily in passenger transport. Modern services give drivers access to a constant flow of orders, a clear payout system and convenient order management on their phone.</p>
<p>If you plan to start working but don&#39;t want to deal with all the details yourself, contact Car City. Our managers will help you:</p>
<ul class="tiles">
<li>Get the documents you need (KIS &quot;ART&quot;, certification, etc.).</li>
<li>Connect to aggregators.</li>
<li>Sort out equipment and requirements.</li>
</ul>
