Taxi localisation law update: which new models now qualify

Contents

  1. What the law changes
  2. Full list of models that meet the requirements as of 1 March 2026
  3. Legal status of cars not on the list
  4. Where to find cars for work?

What the law changes

Under the updated Federal Law FZ‑580 and the related Government decrees, from March 2026 only cars whose production in Russia has reached the required localisation level (measured in points under Decree No. 719) get the right to work as taxis.

The main goal of the changes is to ensure the industry's technological sovereignty and guarantee spare parts for fleet maintenance. For drivers it means one thing: when buying or renting a car, check not only its technical condition but also whether its VIN is in the register of localised products.

Full list of models that meet the requirements as of 1 March 2026

At the moment the Ministry of Industry and Trade register includes the following brands and models, which are made at Russian plants with full or deep production cycles and are suitable for taxi work:

  1. JSC «АвтоВАЗ» (Togliatti, Izhevsk): Lada Vesta, Lada Largus, Lada Aura.
  2. The «Москвич» plant (Moscow): Москвич 3, Москвич 3e, Москвич 6.
  3. Haval (plant in the Tula region): Haval Jolion, Haval M6, Haval F7.
  4. Avtotor LLC (Kaliningrad): BAIC, Kaiyi.
  5. AGR car plant (Saint Petersburg, the former Hyundai plant): Solaris HS (similar to Hyundai Solaris), Solaris HC (similar to Hyundai Creta), Solaris KRS / KRX (similar to Kia Rio / Rio X‑Line).
  6. Motorinvest LLC (Lipetsk region): Evolute i‑Pro, Evolute i‑Joy, i‑Sky.

And others.

Legal status of cars not on the list

If a foreign-built car (for example, a "pure-blood" Korean one) got into the taxi register before 1 March 2026, there's no need to panic. You have every right to keep working on this car until the current licence expires.

What's more, the law lets you keep renewing the licence for the same car if it was already in the register when the amendments came into force. So nobody is going to "kick" old, proven foreign cars out of the taxi business.

From 1 March 2026 the rules for registering new cars as taxis have changed. Now the key criterion is localisation points.

— Nikita Mironov, head of the rent-to-own department
