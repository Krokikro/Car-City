<p>In one year of taxi work a car covers as much distance as an ordinary owner drives in five to seven years. With this kind of use, reliability and access to service matter more than the brand and country of origin. Let&#39;s look at which Chinese models have kept up with this pace in Russian fleets.</p>
<h2 id="chery-tiggo-4-and-tiggo-4-pro">Chery Tiggo 4 and Tiggo 4 Pro</h2><p>The most common Chinese taxi cars in Russia are the Tiggo series models. Chery was one of the first to localise production and spare parts distribution in Russia, so maintenance is easy to get and its cost is predictable.</p>
<p>Tiggo 4 is a compact crossover with a basic naturally aspirated 113 horsepower engine paired with a CVT. More advanced versions come with a 147 horsepower turbo engine and a DCT6 dual-clutch robotic gearbox. In taxi use it has proven itself as a reliable car:</p>
<ul class="tiles">
<li>the body resists corrosion thanks to galvanising;</li>
<li>the suspension is adapted to Russian roads;</li>
<li>the engine is undemanding with regular maintenance;</li>
<li>spare parts are available in every region of the country.</li>
</ul>
<p>Tiggo 4 Pro is an updated version with a better interior and more equipment. In the Yandex Taxi classifier it gets into the Comfort fare class, which increases income from each trip.</p>
<h2 id="belgee-x50">Belgee X50</h2><p>Belgee is a joint production of Geely and a Belarusian plant in Zhodino. Technically it&#39;s a Geely Coolray assembled in Belarus, which gives several practical advantages: deliveries come without customs barriers, the parts are compatible with a wide range of Geely models, and the local production status makes some paperwork easier.</p>
<p>X50 is a compact city crossover with a 1.5 l, 150 hp turbo engine paired with a robotic gearbox. In the Yandex Taxi classifier it gets into Comfort. A warranty of 5 years or 150 000 km is rare in this price segment and a clear sign that the maker is confident in the reliability of the parts.</p>
<p>For taxi work the X50 is good in the city in particular: it&#39;s agile, economical (about 8 l/100 km in the city cycle) and convenient for short and medium trips.</p>
<h2 id="belgee-x70">Belgee X70</h2><p>X70 is the larger crossover in the same Belgee range, based on the Geely Atlas platform. It&#39;s much bigger than the X50: the wheelbase is longer, the back row is noticeably roomier and the sound insulation is better. These are exactly the qualities that put the X70 into Comfort+: passengers who pay more expect a different feel from the trip.</p>
<p>Specifications:</p>
<ul class="tiles">
<li>engine: turbo, 177 hp;</li>
<li>gearbox: 7DCT (dual-clutch robotic);</li>
<li>fuel consumption: 6,8 l/100 km combined;</li>
<li>0–100 km/h: 9,9 seconds.</li>
</ul>
<p>In terms of reliability, the X70 inherits the reputation of the Geely Atlas, one of Geely&#39;s most widespread and proven platforms. The engine runs smoothly, and the gearbox causes no complaints in city use. With timely maintenance the key parts last 150–200 thousand km.</p>
<h2 id="how-to-choose-a-chinese-car-for-taxi-work">How to choose a Chinese car for taxi work</h2><p>The most reliable Chinese cars for taxi work in Russia have several things in common:</p>
<ul class="tiles">
<li>localised production or assembly, which cuts delivery times and spare parts costs;</li>
<li>service available in the city, which matters with high mileage;</li>
<li>the model getting into the right Yandex Taxi fare class in the classifier.</li>
</ul>
<p>After three years of use in Russian taxi fleets, four reliable Chinese taxi cars stand out: Chery Tiggo 4, Chery Tiggo 4 Pro, Belgee X50 and Belgee X70. All four are in the Car City fleet.</p>
<p>The best Chinese taxi cars in the Comfort segment are Belgee X50 and Chery Tiggo 4 Pro: proven platforms, modern equipment and an interior passengers like. Chinese cars in this class now make up most of the taxi car rental market in Moscow. Belgee X70 for the Comfort+ class offers more space, runs quieter and better meets premium expectations.</p>
<p>Which Chinese car to choose for taxi work and which Chinese brand is the most reliable depends a lot on the fare class and your work schedule. Chery is steadily ahead in the number of cars in fleets and in well-organised maintenance. Belgee (Geely) is a strong alternative with long service life and attractive warranty terms.</p>
<p>All the models listed are available in the Car City fleet with the first day of rent free, 24/7 technical support and handover on the day you apply.</p>
