OSGOP for taxis: what it is, how much it costs and how to get it

Contents

  1. What is OSGOP
  2. Who needs OSGOP insurance
  3. What an OSGOP policy covers
  4. How to get OSGOP
  5. What happens if you don't get OSGOP

What is OSGOP

OSGOP is compulsory civil liability insurance for carriers, regulated by Federal Law No. 67‑FZ of 14.06.2012. The insurance requirement for taxis was introduced by Federal Law No. 278‑FZ of 24.06.2023.

While OSAGO covers the driver's liability to third parties, OSGOP protects the life, health and property of the people inside the car. Even if the accident wasn't the taxi driver's fault, the passenger still gets a payout from the carrier's insurance company.

Who needs OSGOP insurance

Everyone who legally carries passengers for money must get the policy: big taxi fleets, small transport companies, sole proprietors (IP) and self-employed drivers. If a person takes orders through an aggregator and gets paid for the trip, they're considered a carrier. And that means they're responsible for passengers' safety.

Who should get the policy: the fleet or the driver

OSGOP is taken out by whoever holds the licence. If the driver rents a car from a taxi fleet, the fleet gets the insurance. In that case the driver must carry a copy of the document. If the driver owns the car and the licence is in their own name, they must buy OSGOP themselves.

Do the self-employed need OSGOP

Paying the professional income tax doesn't cancel a carrier's duties. If something happens to a passenger during a trip, the self-employed driver will be held responsible. An OSGOP policy is the only protection against recourse claims and debts of millions of rubles.

What an OSGOP policy covers

The insurance contract covers three types of damage. The payout limits are set by Art. 8 of Law No. 67‑FZ. A carrier can choose any licensed insurer, but the compensation amounts are the same everywhere:

  • Death: a fixed payout of 2 million rubles. The money goes to the heirs or dependants of the deceased.
  • Harm to health: up to 2 million rubles. The final payout depends on how serious the injuries are and is calculated using a table approved by the Russian Government.
  • Damage to property: up to 23 thousand rubles. Covers damage to personal belongings: a broken phone or laptop, torn clothes or damaged luggage.

Important: OSGOP pays out no matter who caused the accident. The passenger gets compensation in any case.

Harm to health

The maximum amount is two million rubles. Doctors determine the amount: each injury has its own percentage of the limit. For example, a concussion is rated at 3–5 % (60–100 thousand rubles), a broken limb at 7–15 % (140–300 thousand rubles). If there are several injuries, the percentages are added up.

Death

If a passenger dies, the insurer pays 2 025 000 rubles. To claim, you'll need the death certificate and documents confirming the right to inherit.

Damage to property

The limit for this risk is 23 000 rubles. To get a payout, the passenger must take photos of the damage and make sure the damaged items are listed in the traffic police (GIBDD) report or in the incident document the carrier is required to issue.

How to get OSGOP

The procedure takes very little time and can all be done online. The steps are the same for taxi fleets and private carriers:

  1. Choose an insurance company. You can only work with companies that are members of the National Union of Liability Insurers (NSSO). The current list is on the union's official website, nsso.ru.
  2. Prepare the documents. You'll need an application, a copy of the STS for each car and a copy of a valid passenger transport licence.
  3. Pay for the policy. The price is regulated by the Central Bank. The rate range is from 1,3 to 5,4 thousand rubles a year per car. The average market price is about 3,5 thousand rubles.
  4. Enter the details in the register. After payment, the policy arrives by email. The contract details must be sent to the register of carriers through Gosuslugi or an MFC public service centre.

On 1 September 2026, Federal Law No. 95‑FZ of 09.04.2026 comes into force. It allows the self-employed and sole proprietors to take out short-term OSGOP policies, for as little as one day. Insurers must apply reducing coefficients, so the price will be in proportion to the policy term. This is handy for drivers who work seasonally or only at weekends.

What happens if you don't get OSGOP

The consequences fall into three groups: financial, professional and personal. Each can knock a driver off their schedule for a long time.

  • Fines. Under Article 11.31 of the Russian Code of Administrative Offences (KoAP), not having a policy is punished: for officials and sole proprietors, from 40 000 to 50 000 rubles; for companies, from 500 000 to 1 000 000 rubles. The traffic police (GIBDD) and Rostransnadzor can check for insurance.
  • Licence suspension. Under clause 4 of Art. 8 of Federal Law No. 580‑FZ, without a valid policy the permit is suspended for 30 days. If the carrier doesn't get OSGOP in that time, the licence is cancelled. Aggregators check the register every day and stop giving orders to drivers with an invalid permit.
  • Recourse claims. If a passenger is hurt in a car without OSGOP, you'll have to pay the compensation out of your own pocket. The amount can reach two million rubles. For most drivers that means years of debt.

Questions and answers

What's the difference between OSGOP and OSAGO for taxis?

OSAGO insures the driver's liability to other road users. OSGOP only protects the passengers in the car. These are two different policies, and having one doesn't remove the duty to get the other.

How can a passenger check whether there's OSGOP?

Enter the car's licence plate on the NSSO website. The system will show whether there's a valid policy and which company issued it.

Can I get OSGOP for a few days?

From 1 September 2026, the self-employed and sole proprietors will be able to buy a policy for as little as one day. Companies still have to insure for at least a year.

Does driving experience or accident history affect the OSGOP price?

No, they don't. Unlike OSAGO, where the price depends a lot on the bonus-malus coefficient and the driver's experience, OSGOP rates are set by the Central Bank for all carriers. Neither past accidents, nor the driver's age, nor the region of work affect the OSGOP price.

How to save on OSGOP in 2026?

If you want to save on the policy, choose your insurance company carefully. Although the rate range is the same for everyone, each insurer sets its own price within it. The difference can be several thousand rubles. It's easy to compare current offers on comparison websites or directly on the websites of insurers that are NSSO members.

Can OSGOP costs be counted when calculating tax?

Yes, sole proprietors on the simplified tax system include the cost of the OSGOP policy in their business expenses, which lowers their taxable base. Self-employed people paying the professional income tax don't get this deduction, but their rates are already low: 4 % on income from private individuals and 6 % from companies.

Can I get money back for the policy if the car broke down or stopped working as a taxi?

Yes, if the OSGOP contract is ended early, part of the amount paid is returned to the carrier. The refund is calculated based on how long the policy was in force. Grounds for a refund are stopping taxi work or a breakdown that makes the car unusable.
