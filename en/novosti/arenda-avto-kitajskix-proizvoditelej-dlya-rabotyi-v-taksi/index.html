Renting Chinese-made cars for taxi work

Renting cars from China for taxi work

Renting Chinese cars for taxi work is becoming more and more popular thanks to their many advantages.

Chinese carmakers offer a wide range of models from Economy and Comfort up to premium class, from compact sedans to crossovers and roomy minivans.

At Carcity you can get cars from leading Chinese brands such as Chery, Geely, Exeed, Haval and others. Each has its own features, style and technology. This lets you pick a rental car that fits your preferences and budget, for every taste and wallet.

Competitive rental prices make these cars affordable for most drivers. Chinese cars are cheap to maintain, which matters a lot for taxi drivers. Most models are designed with Russian operating conditions in mind, so they cope well with different climates and road conditions.

This gives a high level of comfort for both the driver and the passengers.
