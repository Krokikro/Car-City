Features of Comfort class cars

Comfort class taxi cars for rent in Moscow, rent-to-own: Chery Tiggo, Geely, Москвич at CarCity.

When choosing a car for Comfort class taxi work, pay attention to its running costs and reliability. The preference goes to cars that handle frequent city trips well and have low fuel consumption.

Chery, Geely and Москвич models offer modern features that help drivers handle demanding routes more easily and keep costs under control.

In the Comfort class, convenience and comfort for passengers are especially important.

When choosing cars for this category, keep in mind the main requirements for taxi fleet cars: a roomy, comfortable interior, adjustable and heated seats, climate control, modern navigation devices, adjustable suspension for a smooth ride and quality trim materials. A big boot for passengers' belongings and reliable safety systems are also needed.

Among the best Comfort class cars are Chery Tiggo 4, Toyota Camry and Kia K5, which stand out for their stylish design and high level of comfort. Spare parts for Chinese models are easy to find.

Requirements for Comfort taxi drivers

Comfort class taxi drivers are expected to look neat, speak properly, help passengers with luggage and check the car for forgotten items.

Kia K5 for taxi work

A serious competitor to the Camry. The model's success comes from its balance: a lot of car and features for less money, and the new generation K5 also has an attractive, aggressive design. Besides, the rear seat in the K5 is comfortable, and the space for the passenger in the right rear seat can be adjusted with buttons on the back of the front seat, a feature typical of the premium segment.
