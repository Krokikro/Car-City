<h2 id="advantages-of-renting-a-taxi-car">Advantages of renting a taxi car</h2><p>In big cities taxi services are in high demand.</p>
<p>Each company that rents out cars may have its own set of terms. Pay attention to the following:</p>
<ol class="stepl">
<li>How much time a day the rental covers.</li>
</ol>
<p>Some taxi services rent out a car for a few hours, others for a full day. A daily rental costs more. But the car is also available at any hour of the day or season.</p>
<ol start="2">
<li>Getting a waybill and passing the pre-trip medical check.</li>
</ol>
<p>To go on the line you must have all the necessary documents. A traffic police (GIBDD) officer may check them, and if you don&#39;t have them you&#39;ll be fined. The car owner may do the pre-trip medical check at the taxi company or let the driver do it on their own.</p>
<ol start="3">
<li>Fuel, car washes, parking and fees.</li>
</ol>
<p>These are the renter&#39;s costs.</p>
<h2 id="advantages-of-renting-a-taxi-car-at-car-city">Advantages of renting a taxi car at Car City:</h2><ul class="ticks">
<li>You don&#39;t need any extra investment to start working. Right after signing the contract you can start taking orders and earning. All you need is to get the car keys</li>
</ul>
<p>With Car City you can get a car on the same day you fill in the request. There is no non-refundable deposit.</p>
<h3>Rent-to-own: a smart alternative</h3>
<p>With rent-to-own you pay from 1 850 ₽/day. After 36 months the car becomes yours. It is better than a car loan: 7 days of holiday every six months, repairs paid in instalments, payments frozen after an accident.</p>
<ul class="ticks">
<li>Car maintenance is the taxi fleet&#39;s responsibility. You don&#39;t need to spend your own money on maintenance. All scheduled repairs and maintenance are done by the taxi company.</li>
</ul>
<p>Car City not only does regular maintenance but also cleans your car.</p>
<p>You can report any faults you notice while using the car, and we&#39;ll fix the problem quickly.</p>
<ul class="ticks">
<li>No need to buy or run your own car.</li>
</ul>
<p>When you rent a car at Car City, you get the first day of rental free. Withdraw your money with no limits, at any time.</p>
<ul class="ticks">
<li>You can choose a convenient rental schedule.</li>
</ul>
<p>Car City offers a flexible rental schedule: 5/2, 6/1, 7/0, depending on your needs. In fact, while you rent the car, it&#39;s at your disposal 24/7.</p>
