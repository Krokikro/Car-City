About the Sollers Atlant 1.9 TD

The Sollers Atlant 1.9TD combines practicality with modern industrial design. Straight lines, a powerful front grille and high wheel arches emphasise its reliability and readiness for serious loads. Its look inspires trust – this is a working vehicle built for the job.

The Sollers Atlant interior is plain, functional and thought out to the last detail. An ergonomic dashboard, hard-wearing trim and comfortable seats keep you comfortable even on long shifts. The centre console has a modern multimedia screen and climate controls – everything is at hand, just as it should be in a reliable business partner.

The cabin is surprisingly spacious, with a logically organised workspace. A high seating position, excellent visibility and plenty of storage compartments make every trip convenient and safe. The driver and passenger will appreciate how easy it is to get in and how well the cab is adapted to real needs.
