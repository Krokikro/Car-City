03

Benefits of rent-to-own in the "Economy" class

With rent-to-own you get a balance between price, comfort and the chance to become an owner without overpaying. Here's why this format is popular with taxi drivers:

  1. Minimal investment at the start. The minimum down payment is only 80 000 rubles, and by agreement with Car City managers it can be split in half.
  2. Gradual buyout without banks or leasing. All payments go directly — no middlemen, interest or hidden terms. You control the buyout process yourself.
  3. Reasonable ownership timeline. After 12–24 months the car becomes fully yours. The terms are flexible and depend on the schedule you choose.
  4. Works for sharing with a partner. Want to split shifts or take a rest? You can hand the car over to a partner — no restrictions from our side.
  5. Only cars in good working order. Every car is checked before handover. We provide regular maintenance — no surprises on the road.
  6. Support even in emergencies. If something unexpected happens, you don't lose the car — we'll find a solution so you can keep the buyout going and continue working.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How can I be sure the car will become mine?

We'll give you contacts of customers who have already bought their cars and show you a sample contract at the office or online. Everything is transparent.

What if I can't work for a while — for example, I've gone away or I'm ill?

We understand that life happens. In such cases you can take a holiday — we offer 14 days a year without rent-to-own payments.

I'm not a Russian citizen — can I get a rent-to-own car?

Yes, Car City is open to foreign citizens. We work with drivers from Uzbekistan, Armenia, Tajikistan, Kyrgyzstan, Azerbaijan and other countries. The main thing is having a driver's licence and being ready to work honestly.

Who pays for repairs and maintenance?

Warranty and insurance cases are at our expense. Everything else is by agreement.

Can I choose a specific car model to buy?

Yes, we have a choice of cars in the "Economy" class. You can pick a model you like from those available when you contact us, and our managers will help you find an option that fits your budget and goals.
