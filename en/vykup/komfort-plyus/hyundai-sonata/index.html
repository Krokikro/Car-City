About the Hyundai Sonata

Most of the car's innovations were developed in Korea, where the brand comes from. The hallmarks of the "Comfort+" class Hyundai Sonata sedan are a well-designed interior with plenty of space, maximum safety, convenience and speed.

Those who love comfortable rides with every convenience will definitely appreciate the interior and technical equipment of the Hyundai Sonata taxi. As a renter, you will surely appreciate the practicality, safety and technology of this car.

Rent-to-own a comfortable Hyundai Sonata 2021

During the trip you can lower or raise the mirrors and door windows with one press of a button. Travelling in a Hyundai Sonata is especially pleasant thanks to the modern multimedia system. For your and your passengers' comfort there are firm, ergonomic seats with armrests – both in the front and in the back row. Automatic adjustment lets you sit in any position, which is especially useful on long trips.

Renting this car guarantees reliable taxi trips. The cabin has an airbag system and active headrests. An electronic stability control module is built into the design.
