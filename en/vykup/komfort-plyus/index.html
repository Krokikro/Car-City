If you want to drive a taxi in a solid, modern and reliable car, rent-to-own in the Comfort+ class at Car City is a perfect fit. This format is for those who value comfort, plan to work long-term and want to eventually own the car personally.

We offer cars fully prepared for taxi work: licence, branding wrap and insurance are already included in the terms. All you have to do is get behind the wheel and start earning — knowing that every payment brings you closer to owning the car.

You can choose a buyout period of 12 to 24 months and a payment schedule that fits your workload. We don't require a credit history, don't run bank checks and don't set rigid limits. Working with us means flexibility, support and real benefits.

With Car City you get more than a rent-to-own car — you get a reliable partner. Here are the main reasons drivers choose us:

  • Everything you need for work. The cars already have a licence, aggregator branding and insurance, and have been serviced.
  • Flexible buyout period. From 1 to 2 years, with the option to buy out early with no penalties.
  • No citizenship restrictions. We work with drivers from the CIS and other countries.
  • Real savings. You don't pay separately for the branding wrap, OSAGO or waybills — that saves you up to 100 000 ₽ a year.
  • 14 days of holiday a year. No payment obligations.
  • Work with a partner. You can hand the car over to a partner to boost your income or take a rest.
  • Round-the-clock tech support. Help after accidents, questions about documents and aggregators — consultants are always available.
  • Your contract is kept in an emergency. Even if you temporarily can't pay, we'll find a solution and won't just take the car away.

For more than 7 years we have been providing taxi cars and helping drivers become owners. More than 1500 people have already chosen us, and 300 drivers have successfully bought their cars. We don't just rent out cars — we help you earn steadily, grow and move toward your goal.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What happens if I temporarily can't pay?

We always look for an individual solution: we can give you a payment delay or freeze the contract until you are able to pay again.

How reliable are the cars?

All cars get regular maintenance and are insured, and if something breaks you can count on help from our mechanics.

Can I work with any aggregator?

Yes, you choose who to work with — Yandex, Citymobil and others. The main thing is that the car is used for its intended purpose and the contract terms are followed.

Can I hand the car over to another driver for a shift?

Yes, we don't forbid working with a partner. It helps you buy the car faster and keep your income on days off or during a holiday.

How quickly can I start working after signing up?

Paperwork and handover usually take 1 working day. You can get behind the wheel and start working on the line literally on the day you send your request.
