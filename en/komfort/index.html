Advantages of renting Comfort class cars for taxi work

If you value stability and safety and enjoy longer trips, choose a Comfort class rental car:

  • A car that's comfortable for passengers. These cars usually have a roomy interior with plenty of legroom, comfortable adjustable seats, armrests and other features. Comfort class is most often chosen for longer trips of 30-40 minutes or more, since passengers can relax and rest in these cars.
  • A pleasant interior environment. Great sound insulation comes with good climate control, which makes the ride pleasant even in frost or heavy heat.
  • Easy to drive. Whatever the body type or gearbox, a Comfort class taxi rental car usually has extra driver-assistance systems. It takes minimal effort to drive, so even after several hours of work you'll still feel fresh and full of energy.
  • Price. Renting the car doesn't cost much. The fare doesn't scare customers away either, so you can earn well.

FAQ

Questions and answers

What are the driver requirements for renting a Comfort class car?

The main things are experience and responsibility. To rent, you must be 21 or older and have 3 or more years of driving experience. The only documents you need are a passport, a driver's licence and SNILS.

What is included in a Comfort class taxi car rental?

We give you a car fully ready for work: serviced, insured and with a clean interior. All you need to do is get behind the wheel. If needed, CarCity tech support helps you around the clock.

What should I do if something goes wrong with the car?

It's simple — call our support service, which works 24/7. We'll sort it out: arrange a repair, a replacement car or quick roadside help.

Can I connect to any taxi fleet?

When renting, you can't choose a different taxi fleet. However, these restrictions are lifted if you take our car rent-to-own.
