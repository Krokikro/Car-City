About the Chery Tiggo 4

The Chery Tiggo 4 is one of the Chinese maker's best-selling models. With affordable rent and simple servicing, this crossover lets a taxi driver work without getting tired and carry any passengers, even to places with poor roads. Its compact size and low weight for a crossover improve its driving qualities and help save fuel in the city.

Rent a Chery Tiggo 4 in comfort

The car is not only good for the driver but also comfortable for passengers. The spacious, stylish cabin easily carries four passengers. Add excellent sound insulation and a smooth ride: you'll get lots of positive reviews from customers, which will quickly raise your rating.

Model specifications

  • Engine: Petrol, 1.5 TCI, 147 hp
  • Capacity: 5 seats, 340 L boot
  • Dimensions: 4338x1830x1645 mm, wheelbase – 2630 mm, ground clearance – 171 mm
  • Fuel consumption: 6,6 L in the combined cycle
  • Performance: top speed – 190 km/h, 0 to 100 km/h – 9,7 s
