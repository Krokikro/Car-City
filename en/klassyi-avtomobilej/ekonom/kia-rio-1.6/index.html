Driver requirements

  • Citizenship: To work on our cars you need citizenship of one of these countries: Russia, Belarus, Kyrgyzstan, Kazakhstan, South Ossetia, Abkhazia
  • Age: The minimum age to start working with Car City is 21
  • Experience: To rent a car or take it rent-to-own, you need a driver's licence held for at least 3 years
  • Documents: Driver's licence, passport, KIS "ART" and a certificate of no criminal record
  • Self-employed or IP: The driver must register as self-employed or as a sole proprietor (IP)
