FAQ

Frequently asked questions about rent-to-own

Which Kia models are good for taxi work?

For taxi work drivers most often choose the Kia Rio and Kia Rio X. These models meet aggregator requirements, and they are nimble, fuel-efficient and comfortable for both driver and passengers. They pass exams and fleet checks without problems and perform well in heavy city use.

What is included in the price of a rent-to-own Kia for taxi work?

The price already includes the main and mandatory items:

  • 1-year OSAGO insurance;
  • the option to work with any taxi fleet you choose
  • round-the-clock tech support and roadside help, including after an accident.

You also get a car fully ready for work — no extra spending needed.

How can I be sure I'll actually get the car at the end of rent-to-own?

We have been on the market since 2018, and every month we sign more than 25 cars on rent-to-own. Everything is set out in an official contract. If you like, we can give you contacts of drivers who went all the way and became car owners.

Can a leasing company take a car away from the fleet?

No. Most of the cars belong to us directly. The ones that are leased are protected by individual contracts made for that specific vehicle. All obligations are met.

Can I share a rent-to-own car with a friend?

Yes, you can share work on the car — working as a crew is allowed. The main thing is that the driver meets the basic requirements (experience, documents) and is approved by the manager.

Can I go on holiday in a rent-to-own car?

Yes, just discuss the details with the manager in advance. Some drivers have taken their rent-to-own cars on trips to Sochi and other cities — there are many such cases, and we are fine with it.

How much driving experience do I need?

The minimum is 3 years of experience. You also need: a Russian passport, a category "B" driver's licence and a smartphone with internet to install the apps that send you orders.

If you plan to buy the car for personal use (not for taxi work), the minimum experience may be lower. This is decided case by case — you can discuss it with the managers at the office. We don't check your credit history or ask for unnecessary documents.
