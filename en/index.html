FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I start working with Car City? Driver requirements

To rent a taxi car in Moscow at Car City, you only need to meet a few simple requirements. We don't ask too much, but we care about safe trips.

Citizenship. To work on our cars, you need citizenship of one of these countries: Russia, Belarus, Kyrgyzstan, Kazakhstan, South Ossetia, Abkhazia

Experience. To rent a car or rent-to-own, you need a driver's licence held for at least 3 years

Age. The minimum age to start working with Car City is 21

Documents. Driver's licence, passport, KIS "ART" and a certificate of no criminal record. With the full set of documents you can take a car for taxi work right after signing up.

Will I get a car if other fleets left bad reviews about me?

We look at reviews from other fleets during the check, but the final decision is made by a manager after a personal interview.

What is the fleet commission?

Our fleet commission is 4%. It is one of the most driver-friendly offers among taxi rental fleets.

What is the minimum rental period?

The minimum rental period for any of our cars is 30 days

Can I work for another fleet?

With a standard taxi rent in Moscow — no, the car is assigned to Car City. But if you plan rent-to-own, there are no limits on choosing an aggregator.

Are there any bonuses for current drivers?

Yes, of course. With our "Bring a friend" offer you get 5 thousand rubles for each new driver who signs a rental contract with us, and 15 thousand rubles for a rent-to-own contract.

Where can I rent a car for taxi work?

You can rent a taxi car in Moscow with no middlemen and no hidden fees at the Car City taxi fleet. Our offices and parking lots are at three addresses:

  • Mitino metro (1st Mitinsky lane, 15 bldg. 3);
  • Kuntsevskaya metro (Vitebskaya st., 11);
  • Michurinsky Prospekt metro (Udaltsova st., 36).

If you are looking for a licensed taxi rental with no deposit, you don't have to drive all over Moscow. Come to the nearest Car City office with your documents to rent a taxi car.
